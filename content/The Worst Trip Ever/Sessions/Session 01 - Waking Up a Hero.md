**Act:** Act I – The Illusion Holds  
**Theme:** Confusion, urgency, accidental heroism  
**Mechanics Introduced (Hidden):** Lucidity (narrative-only), no numbers revealed

## Strong Start

The characters awaken **simultaneously**, choking on smoke and heat, sprawled across the common room floor of what they perceive as a roadside inn. Fire crawls up the walls, tables are overturned, and terrified patrons scream for help. None of them remember who they are, how they arrived, or why everything hurts—but their bodies move on instinct.

Outside, shadowy figures smash windows and force their way in as something *roars* from the street. The players hear patrons around them screaming from rooms beside them.

> Do not allow time for introductions. Immediate danger forces action.

---

## Scene 1 – Fire and Instinct

### What the Players See (Fantasy)

- A burning inn under attack
- Innocent patrons trapped upstairs
- Hostile “bandits” forcing entry
- Imminent structural collapse

Players must act immediately to:
- Rescue trapped civilians
- Fight off attackers
- Contain or escape the blaze

Encourage intent-first play. Abilities emerge through instinct.

---

### What Is Actually Happening (DM-Only)

> [!warning] SPOILERS – DO NOT REVEAL TO PLAYERS

The building is **not an inn**.  
It is a **repurposed commercial structure** housing an **experimental, aerosolizable mind-altering drug**.  

A containment failure triggered a chemical fire. The smoke has **aerosolized the compound**, exposing everyone nearby. The PCs are experiencing the most extreme reaction: a shared, fully immersive fantasy hallucination.

Key facts:
- The hallucination is drug-induced, not magical
- Stress and exertion deepen the effect
- Memory suppression is a side effect

This explains:
- The sudden start during crisis
- Shared reality among PCs
- Total amnesia before awakening

---

## Scene 2 – The Attackers Arrive

### What the Players See (Fantasy)

A coordinated group of **[Placeholder Enemy Group / Bandits]** breaches the building, clearly searching for “something valuable that must not be lost.” They fight efficiently but avoid needless slaughter.

Their equipment looks strange—masks, rigid armor, unfamiliar weapons.

---

### What Is Actually Happening (DM-Only)

The “bandits” are a **HAZMAT response team** employed by a corporate or government entity.

Their real objectives:
- Contain the chemical breach
- Prevent civilian exposure
- Secure or destroy evidence of the experiment

Their behavior is shaped by protocol:
- They avoid killing unless absolutely necessary
- They retreat once containment is lost
- They communicate in clipped, urgent phrases

Fantasy translations:
- Breathing apparatus → arcane masks
- Fire retardant foam → frost spells or wards
- ID badges → strange sigils or talismans

---

## Scene 3 – Civilians and Collapse

### What the Players See (Fantasy)

With the attackers driven back or repelled, survivors gather. The fire diminishes. The characters are thanked—awkwardly.

Someone finally asks:

> “Who are you people?”

The party realizes:
- They remember nothing before this moment
- They share the same gap in memory
- Their presence here feels *expected*

---

### What Is Actually Happening (DM-Only)

The “patrons” are residents, workers, or bystanders also exposed to the drug.

- Most are hallucinating in the same world
- Some are partially aware something is wrong
- Fear responses may not match gratitude, depends if they are saved or not

NPC unease reflects reality breaking through.

## Secrets / Clues (DM-Only)

- The fire began before the PCs “woke up”
- The attackers are trying to erase evidence
- The smoke is the actual source of the hallucination
- The PCs were closest to the highest concentration

Reveal slowly. No exposition.

---

## Lucidity Opportunities (Narrative Only)

- A PC near collapse briefly sees the room *without flames*
- A strange mechanical hum replaces the crackle of fire
- A “bandit” speaks a word that doesn’t fit the fantasy

No saves yet. Only description.

## End State / Cliffhanger

The party is offered shelter and answers in the morning.

As they sleep, one character dreams:

> Bright white lights.  
> A flat voice: “Exposure confirmed. We’re too late.”  
> Then heat. Then fire again.

Session ends.

---

## DM Guiding Principle

> The fantasy is not madness—it is a survival response.

Let the heroes feel heroic.
The cost comes later.
