## Strong Start

The characters awaken **simultaneously**, choking on smoke and heat, sprawled across the common room floor of what they perceive as a roadside inn. Fire crawls up the walls, tables are overturned, and terrified patrons scream for help. None of them remember who they are, how they arrived, or why everything hurts—but their bodies move on instinct.

Outside, shadowy figures smash windows and force their way in as something *roars* from the street. The players hear patrons around them screaming from rooms beside them.

> Do not allow time for introductions. Immediate danger forces action.

## Secrets / Clues (DM-Only)

- The fire began before the PCs “woke up”
- The attackers are trying to erase evidence
- The smoke is the actual source of the hallucination
- The PCs were closest to the highest concentration

Reveal slowly. No exposition.

---

## Lucidity Opportunities (Narrative Only)

- A PC near collapse briefly sees the room *without flames*
- A strange mechanical hum replaces the crackle of fire
- A “bandit” speaks a word that doesn’t fit the fantasy

No saves yet. Only description.

---

## End State / Cliffhanger

The party is offered shelter and answers in the morning.

As they sleep, one character dreams:

> Bright white lights.  
> A flat voice: “Exposure confirmed. We’re too late.”  
> Then heat. Then fire again.

Session ends.

---

## DM Guiding Principle

> The fantasy is not madness—it is a survival response.

Let the heroes feel heroic.
The cost comes later.
