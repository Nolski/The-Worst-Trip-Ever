### The Attackers Arrive

A disciplined group of masked bandits storms the burning building, methodically attempting to kill civilians and eliminate the party rather than loot or negotiate, forcing the characters to make fast, heroic decisions under pressure. The attackers’ coordinated tactics and unsettling professionalism set them apart from ordinary raiders, hinting that this threat—and the situation itself—is not what it seems.

### What the Players See (Fantasy)

A coordinated group of [[Bandits - Hazmat Operative Overview]] breaches the building, clearly searching for “something valuable that must not be lost.” They fight efficiently and are killing all in the building.

Their equipment looks strange—masks, rigid armor, unfamiliar weapons.

### Encounter Objective
The party must stop a group of intruders who are ruthlessly attacking civilians and advancing toward the party’s position.  
- **Primary Threat:** Protect civilians  
- **Secondary Threat:** Survive the attack

This combat should feel intense but fair for **four level 1 players** and reinforce urgency without being overwhelming. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}


### Encounter Composition

Use **4 enemies total**, which keeps the encounter in the *Easy to Medium* range for a party of four level 1 characters (each enemy is CR 1/8, similar to Bandits). :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

- **2× Melee Operatives** (close-range attackers)
- **1× Ranged Operative** (support fire)
- **1× Melee or Ranged Operative** (flexible depending on desired difficulty)

This mix gives players a chance to face multiple threats with variety (melee and ranged), helping teach positioning and tactical responses. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

### Enemy Overview (In-Game Perception)

**Fantasy View:**  
A disciplined squad of masked raiders cutting through chaos with lethal precision. They show no mercy toward civilians and do not hesitate to attack enemies—even the party.

**Behavior Highlights:**
- Prioritize killing civilians first
- Focus fire on any PC blocking their path
- Avoid sticking around if severely outnumbered or wounded

> Bandits use simple, direct tactics: attack what’s closest, protect their advance, and treat civilians as obstacles.

---

### Turn-by-Turn Flow (Ideal)

1. **Round 1 – Initiative**
   - Bandits close distance and fire ranged attacks.
   - Civilians scream and begin to flee or hide.
   - Players make their first tactical decisions (move to protect, prepare spells, etc.).

2. **Rounds 2–4 – Combat Develops**
   - **Melee Operatives:** Engage the closest PC or civilian; try to pin them down.
   - **Ranged Operative:** Stays behind cover and uses ranged attacks to pressure the party.
   - Civilians may be moved out of harm’s way by players (role-played or via movement).

3. **Rounds 5+ – Shift in Tactics**
   - If bandits see their advance stalling or face casualties, they may attempt a *withdrawal* (retreat off the field).
   - If PCs are overwhelmed, bandits push harder toward civilians.

This pacing offers **clear phases** and allows new players to observe enemy patterns and learn rules like opportunity attacks, cover, and movement without cognitive overload. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

### Tactical Guidance for the DM

#### Simple Enemy Behavior Patterns
- **Melee Operatives**
  - Close distance quickly
  - Attack the nearest PC
  - If dropped below half HP, fallback to regroup or reposition

- **Ranged Operative**
  - Uses terrain or obstacles for cover
  - Targets injured or slow PCs
  - If threatened in melee, disengages and finds more distance

Keeping enemy behavior predictable helps new players *anticipate consequences and feel smart* when they adapt. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

### Environment & Conditions

Use the collapsed and smoky terrain to teach basic movement rules:
- **Difficult Terrain:** Smoke and debris cost extra movement
- **Partial Cover:** Tables, beams grant +2 to AC and Dexterity saves
- **Hazards:** Falling debris might require a Dexterity save

Adding these elements gives the encounter flavor and teaches straightforward tactical elements without slowing the game.

---

### Player Goals (Clear and Achievable)

- Prevent civilians from dying
- Reduce enemy numbers to zero
- Survive with minimal party damage

These objectives are small, concrete, and help new players focus decisions on what matters in combat.

---

### Aftermath and Transition

When the bandits are defeated or driven off:
- Civilians are shaken but alive
- The authority figure arrives to praise (or question) the party
- Clues (narrative details) about the attackers’ discipline are introduced

This transitions naturally into post-combat roleplay and reinforces that combat outcomes drive the story forward.

### What Is Actually Happening (DM-Only)

The “bandits” are a **HAZMAT response team** employed by a corporate or government entity.

Their real objectives:
- Contain the chemical breach
- Prevent civilian exposure
- Secure or destroy evidence of the experiment

Their behavior is shaped by protocol:
- They avoid killing unless absolutely necessary
- They retreat once containment is lost
- They communicate in clipped, urgent phrases

Fantasy translations:
- Breathing apparatus → arcane masks
- Fire retardant foam → frost spells or wards
- ID badges → strange sigils or talismans
