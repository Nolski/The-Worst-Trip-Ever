**Player Knowledge:**

- Complete amnesia
- World appears as standard fantasy
- No explicit indication of a twist

**DM Goals:**

- Establish tone and tropes
- Build emotional investment
- Seed _barely noticeable_ inconsistencies
- Introduce the hallucination mechanic invisibly

**Narrative Focus:**

- Immediate danger
- Accidental heroism
- Discovery of abilities through instinct

The characters awaken with amnesia in a classic fantasy scenario. The priority here is **hooking the players** and establishing the world. Even without memories, the PCs should have an immediate, **urgent problem** to address – something that forces them to act heroically using their newfound abilities.  For example, the **campaign could begin in medias res**: the party wakes up in a burning tavern or in the middle of a heist-gone-wrong without knowing how they got there. This urgent crisis will launch them into action and cooperation. _During Act I_, the party will likely undertake a short introductory quest (slay some “monsters” or solve a local problem) which doubles as a tutorial for their abilities and the game world. By the end of Act I, they should reach level 2. **Subtle clues** about the true nature of the world can be introduced here but keep the tone mostly light and adventurous.
