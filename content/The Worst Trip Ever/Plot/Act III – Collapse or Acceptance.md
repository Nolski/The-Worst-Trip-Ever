**Player Knowledge:**

- Full awareness of the dual reality
- Understanding that fantasy power is conditional

**DM Goals:**

- Confront the origin of the hallucination
- Force meaningful decisions
- Resolve fantasy and real-world arcs in parallel

**Possible Endings:**

- Escape the illusion and face consequences
- Destroy the mechanism and remain inside fantasy
- Rewrite reality at personal cost

The final act (roughly level 5 content) sees the two realities colliding. By now, the party should be aware of the dual reality mechanic in-game – perhaps they’ve found an NPC or a method to deliberately induce or prevent the hallucinations. They might discover _who_ or _what_ is responsible for their state. This could involve confronting a **big bad** who exists in both worlds: for example, the kindly wizard or patron who guided them in fantasy is revealed as a sinister figure in reality (maybe a crime boss or the very person who drugged them). The final sessions can alternate between fantasy and reality scenes. In fantasy, it might be the archetypal boss battle – slaying a “demon lord” or stopping a cataclysm. In reality, this corresponds to taking down the criminal ringleader or surviving a final desperate situation (like a standoff with law enforcement or the drug’s creators). The PCs’ actions in one world affect the other. Because the PCs are just level 5, avoid making the real-world confrontation _too_ lethal without their powers – perhaps they manage to retain a fragment of their “power” in reality (e.g. the artifact they recovered in fantasy is actually a real-world weapon or tool that can help them). By the end, the players either fully return to reality, having overcome the antagonist and with a chance at redemption, or they might choose to remain lost in the fantasy (a bittersweet ending). Make sure to **resolve the main plot threads** in both realities. The dual ending can be a poignant payoff for the wild adventure.
