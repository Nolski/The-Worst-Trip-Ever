## High-Level Concept

**Fantasy Role:**  
Masked bandits or raiders attempting to retrieve or destroy something “dangerous and valuable.”

**Reality Role:**  
Corporate or government HAZMAT response personnel tasked with containing an experimental psychoactive compound, minimizing exposure, and covering up the incident.

**Narrative Purpose:**  
- Establish early antagonists who are *not truly evil*
- Create moral ambiguity once the truth is revealed
- Provide recurring figures whose actions make more sense in retrospect

---

## Visual Description

### Fantasy Perception
- Heavy cloaks or leather armor
- Masked faces with strange glass lenses
- Tubes, canisters, and metal gear mistaken for arcane tools
- Cold, disciplined movements

### Reality Truth
- Fire-resistant HAZMAT suits
- Full-face respirators with HUD displays
- Breathing hoses and filtration units
- Identification badges and comms equipment

> Their “arcane masks” always obscure their faces — even when damaged.

---

## Behavior & Tactics

### In Combat (Fantasy View)
- Fight efficiently, not recklessly
- Prioritize specific areas or objects over killing
- Retreat once objectives are compromised
- Use area-control effects (smoke, suppression)

### In Reality
- Follow strict protocol
- Avoid lethal force unless absolutely necessary
- Attempt extraction over engagement
- Disengage when contamination risk becomes unacceptable

## Melee Variant – Close Support Operative

**Armor Class:** 12 (resistant overcoat)  
**Hit Points:** 11 (2d8+2)  
**STR** 11 (+0)  **DEX** 12 (+1)  **CON** 12 (+1)  **INT** 10 (+0)  **WIS** 10 (+0)  **CHA** 10 (+0)

### Actions
**Baton Strike (Fantasy Scimitar / Reality Collapsible Tool)**  
Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
Hit: 4 (1d6+1) bludgeoning or slashing damage.

**Reinforced Shield Bash (If using a shield)**  
Melee Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
Hit: 3 (1d4+1) bludgeoning damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 11 Strength saving throw or be pushed 5 ft.

### Role in Encounter
- Engages PCs in melee
- Attempts to protect specialists
- Withdraws if heavily wounded

## Ranged Variant – Suppression Operative

**Armor Class:** 12 (light ballistic gear)  
**Hit Points:** 11 (2d8+2)  
**STR** 10 (0)  **DEX** 12 (+1)  **CON** 12 (+1)  **INT** 10 (+0)  **WIS** 10 (+0)  **CHA** 10 (+0)

### Actions
**Non-Lethal Launcher (Fantasy Light Crossbow / Reality Projectile Suppressor)**  
Ranged Weapon Attack: +3 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target.  
Hit: 5 (1d8+1) piercing or stun rounds.

**Suppressive Fire** *(Recharge 5–6)*  
The operative can fire at two different targets within range, making two separate Non-Lethal Launcher attacks. On a hit, the target must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or be **slowed** (speed halved) until the end of its next turn.

### Role in Encounter
- Fires from cover
- Seeks high ground or line of sight
- Withdraws under pressure

## Magic Variant – Protocol Specialist

**Armor Class:** 12 (gear-embedded shielding)  
**Hit Points:** 11 (2d8+2)  
**STR** 10 (0)  **DEX** 12 (+1)  **CON** 12 (+1)  **INT** 12 (+1)  **WIS** 10 (+0)  **CHA** 10 (+0)

### Spellcasting (Fantasy Perception)
The Protocol Specialist is a 1st-level spellcaster (spell save DC 11, +3 to hit with spell attacks).  
It has the following spells prepared:

- **Cantrips (at will):** *Mage Hand*, *Light*
- **1st level (2 slots):** *Shield*, *Protection from Good and Evil*

*(In reality, these are interpreted through tech interfaces or gear enemies misperceive as magic effects.)*

### Actions
**Pulse Disruptor** *(Ranged Tech Effect)*  
Ranged Spell Attack: +3 to hit, range 60 ft., one target.  
Hit: 4 (1d8) radiant or energy damage (described as “arcane discharge”).

### Role in Encounter
- Supports operatives with defensive tech
- Creates battlefield control
- Avoids extended close combat

---

### Actions

**Scimitar (Fantasy) / Baton or Tool (Reality)**  
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target  
*Hit:* 4 (1d6+1) slashing damage

**Light Crossbow (Fantasy) / Non-lethal Launcher (Reality)**  
*Ranged Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, range 80/320 ft.

## Special Traits (Narrative)

### Protocol Discipline
- Will always disengage if command authority calls retreat
- Will not pursue fleeing PCs beyond objective perimeter

### Masked Anonymity
- PCs never see a face beneath the mask
- Voices may sound distorted or flat

### Wrongness Trigger
- PCs near Lucidity loss may notice:
  - No screams of pain
  - Mechanical coordination
  - Uniform movement patterns

---

## Interaction Notes

- Rarely taunt or gloat
- Speak in statements, not insults
- May shout phrases like:
  - “Secure the breach.”
  - “Abort. Abort.”
  - “Exposure levels rising.”

These should feel *odd*, not overtly modern.

---

## Use Across the Campaign

### Act I
- Primary antagonists
- Appear threatening and mysterious

### Act II
- Reappear in new locations
- PCs may recognize patterns or symbols
- Behavior begins to contradict fantasy logic

### Act III
- Truth revealed
- May become:
  - Secondary antagonists
  - Reluctant allies
  - Victims of the same system

---

## Reality Clues Tied to This NPC

- Their gear resists fire unnaturally well
- They communicate silently at times
- They are never seen looting
- They never celebrate victory

---

## DM Guiding Rule

> These are not villains — they are cleanup.

Play them as professionals doing a grim job, not as moustache-twirling enemies.  
The tragedy lands harder when the truth comes out.
