## High-Level Concept

**Fantasy Role:**  
Masked bandits or raiders attempting to retrieve or destroy something “dangerous and valuable.”

**Reality Role:**  
Corporate or government HAZMAT response personnel tasked with containing an experimental psychoactive compound, minimizing exposure, and covering up the incident.

**Narrative Purpose:**  
- Establish early antagonists who are *not truly evil*
- Create moral ambiguity once the truth is revealed
- Provide recurring figures whose actions make more sense in retrospect

---

## Visual Description

### Fantasy Perception
- Heavy cloaks or leather armor
- Masked faces with strange glass lenses
- Tubes, canisters, and metal gear mistaken for arcane tools
- Cold, disciplined movements

### Reality Truth
- Fire-resistant HAZMAT suits
- Full-face respirators with HUD displays
- Breathing hoses and filtration units
- Identification badges and comms equipment

> Their “arcane masks” always obscure their faces — even when damaged.

---

## Behavior & Tactics

### In Combat (Fantasy View)
- Fight efficiently, not recklessly
- Prioritize specific areas or objects over killing
- Retreat once objectives are compromised
- Use area-control effects (smoke, suppression)

### In Reality
- Follow strict protocol
- Avoid lethal force unless absolutely necessary
- Attempt extraction over engagement
- Disengage when contamination risk becomes unacceptable

## Special Traits (Narrative)

### Protocol Discipline
- Will always disengage if command authority calls retreat
- Will not pursue fleeing PCs beyond objective perimeter

### Masked Anonymity
- PCs never see a face beneath the mask
- Voices may sound distorted or flat

### Wrongness Trigger
- PCs near Lucidity loss may notice:
  - No screams of pain
  - Mechanical coordination
  - Uniform movement patterns

---

## Interaction Notes

- Rarely taunt or gloat
- Speak in statements, not insults
- May shout phrases like:
  - “Secure the breach.”
  - “Abort. Abort.”
  - “Exposure levels rising.”

These should feel *odd*, not overtly modern.

---

## Use Across the Campaign

### Act I
- Primary antagonists
- Appear threatening and mysterious

### Act II
- Reappear in new locations
- PCs may recognize patterns or symbols
- Behavior begins to contradict fantasy logic

### Act III
- Truth revealed
- May become:
  - Secondary antagonists
  - Reluctant allies
  - Victims of the same system

---

## Reality Clues Tied to This NPC

- Their gear resists fire unnaturally well
- They communicate silently at times
- They are never seen looting
- They never celebrate victory

---

## DM Guiding Rule

> These are not villains — they are cleanup.

Play them as professionals doing a grim job, not as moustache-twirling enemies.  
The tragedy lands harder when the truth comes out.
