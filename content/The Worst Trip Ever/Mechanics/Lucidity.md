### Summary

**Lucidity** represents a character’s ability to remain submerged in the hallucination.
This mechanic is optional but recommended for longer arcs.

---

### Lucidity Value

Each character has:

`Lucidity: 3 (maximum)`

Lucidity is tracked secretly by the DM until revealed.

---

### Losing Lucidity

A character loses 1 Lucidity when:

- They fail a Reality Fracture Check by **5 or more**
- They experience a Reality Intrusion at 0 HP
- They directly acknowledge contradictions (“This isn’t real”)

---

### Lucidity Threshold Effects

|Lucidity|Effect|
|---|---|
|3|Hallucination stable|
|2|Intrusions are vivid and emotional|
|1|Real-world details persist after intrusions|
|0|Full Awakening possible|

---

### Recovering Lucidity

Lucidity may be restored by:

- Completing a **Long Rest** (PHB p.186) _under reinforcing conditions_
- Fantasy-aligned reinforcement (praise, ritual, “blessings”)
- External intervention (drugs, magic, NPC action)

Lucidity **cannot exceed 3**.

## Lucidity Checks (Single Unified Roll)

### When to Call for a Check

When a character experiences intense stress, contradiction, or trauma, the DM may call for:

`Wisdom Saving Throw`

**DC Guidelines**

- Early campaign: DC 10
- Mid campaign: DC 12
- Late campaign: DC 14

(Uses standard saving throw rules, PHB p.179)

---

### Outcomes

**Success**

- No mechanical penalty
- Describe brief sensory distortion

**Failure**

- Lose 1 Lucidity
- If at Lucidity 1, also apply a minor mechanical disruption (see below)

---

### Minor Mechanical Disruption (Lucidity 1 Only)

Choose **one**, lasting no longer than **1 round**:

- Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks
- Lose reaction until next turn
- Movement speed reduced to 0 until end of turn

Uses standard disadvantage and reaction rules
