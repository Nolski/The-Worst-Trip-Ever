### Summary

[[**Reintegration**]] is the process by which an Awakened or unstable character is pulled back into the fantasy hallucination through rest, reassurance, denial, or external intervention. When reintegrated, the character regains their class abilities, resets to a low Lucidity state, and retains only fragmented, dreamlike memories of the real world, often accompanied by lingering exhaustion..

---

### Reintegration Triggers

A character may return to the fantasy when **any one** of the following occurs:

- Being knocked out by party members and "healed"
- Receiving reassurance, validation, or purpose within the fantasy
- Being administered The Drug

---

### Mechanical Effects of Reintegration

Upon reintegration:

- Lucidity resets to **1**
- Character gains **one level of Exhaustion**
- Fantasy perception fully returns
- Memories of reality become fragmented and dreamlike
