## Campaign Hub

**System:** D&D 5e  
**Levels:** 1 → 5  
**Tone:** Classic fantasy → psychological horror → dark satire  
**Themes:** Identity, power, denial, consequence, unreliable reality  
**Core Twist:** The fantasy world is a drug-induced hallucination. Players do not know this initially.

## Campaign Premise

The characters awaken with **no memory of who they are or how they arrived** in a familiar fantasy setting. They possess strange competence, instinctively wielding weapons, spells, and skills they do not remember learning.

Over time, it becomes clear that the world behaves _almost_ like a traditional fantasy setting—but not quite. Small inconsistencies appear. Moments of stress cause flashes of an entirely different reality.

Unbeknownst to the characters:

- The fantasy world is a **drug-induced hallucination**
- Their “heroic” deeds are **real-world crimes**
- Their power exists only inside the delusion

This campaign explores how long people can live inside a lie when the lie makes them feel important.
